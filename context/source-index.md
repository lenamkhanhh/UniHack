## Distinguishing evidence types

- The website, Luma, and Builder Hub pages are public organizer/program sources.
- The participant team and Builder Pass status in `competition-context.md` came from an authenticated dashboard. It is summarized without member names, contact details, invite links, email addresses, or pending applicant identities.
- Email review surfaced the same public links for Opening Event logistics, photos, slides, and the next workshop. No inbox message bodies, personal addresses, or private team contacts are reproduced in this repository.
