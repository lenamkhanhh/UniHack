# Competition context

**Research snapshot:** 4 November 2026 (Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh)

## What it is

Avalanche Team1 Vietnam UniHack is a 30-day program for university students in Vietnam to turn an idea into a working product on Avalanche. The program runs from 2 to 30 November 2026. Team1’s website describes it as free and open to students from any major. Participants can build alone or in teams of up to three. They can participate online; only Top 10 finalist teams are expected to pitch and demo in person at the Final Demo Day.

At the time of the live site inspection, the overview showed **586 participants, 176 teams, and 20 suggested ideas**. These counts can change.

The four suggested tracks are:

1. Social finance
2. Game on L1
3. AI agents and security
4. Agent economy on x402

The topics are suggestions, not a required challenge list. Teams can select one, combine ideas, or build their own project. The project should run on one of these Avalanche environments:

- An Avalanche L1 created by the team
- Fuji testnet
- Avalanche C-Chain mainnet

The site notes that Fuji can be used without real money; C-Chain mainnet transactions require AVAX for gas.

## Submission requirements and judging

The detailed Team1 FAQ says to submit through the Team1 website. Any team member can submit on behalf of the team. A draft does not count until it is submitted. The FAQ says only the team and organizers can see the submission.

Each team should prepare:

- A GitHub repository, public or private. For a private repository, add the organizer’s GitHub account `tuantqse90` as a collaborator.
- Pitch slides, such as a Canva or Google Slides link.
- A 2–3 minute demo video.
- Any other supporting material.

The FAQ warns that every link must be accessible to judges; it says inaccessible material can disqualify a team.

Team1’s 100-point rubric:

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---:|
| Technical implementation | 25 |
| Avalanche integration | 25 |
| Creativity and innovation | 15 |
| Impact and use case | 15 |
| Presentation and pitching | 10 |
| UI/UX and demo | 10 |

## Prizes

The headline pool is **$3,500**:

- **$3,000** for the ten finalist teams, approximately 75,000,000 VND in total: 30,000,000 VND first prize, 20,000,000 VND second prize, 11,000,000 VND third prize, and seven encouragement prizes of 2,000,000 VND each.
- **$500 Builder Pass minigame**: 50 builders are drawn to share it ($10 each). The page describes this as separate from the main hackathon prizes and requires five quests to enter the draw. Winners are announced on Final Demo Day.

## Official source conflicts

The official pages inspected do not agree on key logistics:

| Item | Team1 website and Luma | Avalanche Builder Hub listing |
|---|---|---|
| Submission deadline | 21 Nov 2026, 23:59 Vietnam time | 25 Nov 2026, 05:59 Asia/Bangkok |
| Finalist announcement | 23 Nov | 27 Nov |
| Final Demo Day venue | Greenwich Vietnam, 20 Cộng Hòa | University of Tôn Đức Thắng |
| Submission route | Team1 says submit on team1vn.xyz | Builder Hub says submit through build.avax.network |

The Team1 website and Luma agree with each other, while the Builder Hub event page gives the later date, different venue, and different submission route. Treat **21 November** as the conservative planning deadline and confirm the final instructions with the organizers. The Team1 dashboard also says Builder Hub uses a separate account and asks participants to register there; whether this means a separate event registration, a second submission, or both is unclear from the pages reviewed.
